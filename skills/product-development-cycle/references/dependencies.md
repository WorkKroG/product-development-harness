# Capability and Dependency Boundaries

Check only the capability needed by the current dependent stage. Record the actual capability name and version when observable; a catalog or README claim is not availability proof. Specialist presence is not PASS: review its output against the gate and current risk.

| Capability | Purpose | Needed when | Detection | Allowed fallback | Block the gate when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PM | Produce decision-grade problem, user, positioning, journey, scope, requirements, stories, or prioritization evidence | The current discovery or requirements gate needs that evidence | Make one bounded check for an applicable PM capability and record its observable identity or limitation | Use the corresponding explicit harness reference or rubric with available general capability; record the missing specialization and limitation | Required decision-grade product evidence cannot be produced, or project policy requires the specialist |
| Challenge | Independently expose weak PRD, scope, permission, recovery, evidence, and metric assumptions | Gate 7 or another explicitly independent challenge is due | Check once for the current challenge capability and independence | Use an explicit challenge rubric with a separate review pass and record the limitation | Critical questions cannot be resolved, assigned a validation plan, or accepted by the proper authority |
| Finance | Support the current viability or investment decision with proportionate economic evidence | Gate 3.5 or Gate 8 depends on economics | Check once for the relevant finance and spreadsheet capability and record what is actually available | Follow the active financial reference: light Gate 3.5 or investment-proportionate Gate 8; do not revive mandatory early workbooks, exact CAC/LTV, or universal Month-24 targets | Decision-grade economics cannot be produced safely; keep Finance open rather than treating generic output as PASS |
| UX | Produce and review applicable flows, states, responsiveness, and accessibility | Gate 9 or a user-facing change depends on UX evidence | Check once for the needed design, inspection, or accessibility capability | Use the lifecycle UX criteria and repository-native artifacts when they can produce decision-grade evidence | Critical behavior or accessibility cannot be designed or verified for the dependent transition |
| Security | Assess identity, authorization, sensitive data, privacy, abuse, secrets, migration, and recovery | Actual exposure makes Gate 10 or a later transition security-dependent | Check once for the required security expertise or tool and record its observable scope | Use the explicit risk rubric only when available general capability can address the actual risk; retain the limitation | Generic work cannot reach decision-grade evidence, or the risk requires unavailable expertise; keep Security open |
| Planning | Compare architecture and delivery approaches and produce a testable approved plan | Gate 11 or 12 requires technical or delivery planning | Check once for the applicable planning capability | Use the lifecycle architecture and delivery criteria with a documented independent review | Stable contracts, migration, rollback, or acceptance boundaries cannot be made decision-ready |
| Review | Independently assess the exact plan, change, or integrated module against binding sources | PLAN, Change Review, or FINAL is required | Confirm an independent reviewer and exact candidate identity are available | A separate general review may use the explicit rubric if independence and sufficient reasoning are preserved | Independence, exact-head evidence, or decision-grade coverage cannot be established |
| Verification | Produce fresh mechanical, behavioral, manual, security, or operational evidence | Gate 14, release preparation, or a work-item exit depends on checks | Check once for the specific test or inspection capability required by the current risk | Use another reproducible check only when it tests the same observable contract and record the limitation | Required evidence cannot be produced safely or a mandated check remains unavailable |

An Allowed fallback is valid only when an explicit harness reference or rubric plus available general capability can produce decision-grade evidence proportional to scope and risk. Record the absent specialization and the fallback's limitation. Block the gate when decision-grade evidence cannot be produced safely, an explicit user or project policy requires the missing specialist, or the risk or decision needs evidence or expertise the fallback cannot supply. Security and Finance stay open when general capability cannot reach that threshold. **BLOCKED is not PASS.** Missing capability blocks only the dependent gate or transition, not unrelated authorized work.

**Do not install dependencies silently**. Installation, plugin connection, account access, model substitution, or another external change requires its own applicable mandate and platform permission.
